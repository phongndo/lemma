Its successful process exit **does not** mean the evidence chain passed. The expanded single-field variant narrows this further to one `source_commit` field.

`diagnostic.py` exercises real CLI entry points with 112 independent mutations/control cases, plus capture-count/reuse and sidecar cases. Every case begins with a new deep copy. The component-positive control includes all 79 paired metric IDs, all 80 absolute check IDs and the nine scenario labels. `composition.py` separately exercises:

## Bounded review procedure for future decision-support captures

This procedure is usable as a fail-closed checklist now. **An affirmative outcome remains blocked on collection/tooling preparation and selected instruments.** Do not reconstruct missing historical custody by trusting report labels. An absent required record stops review with `invalid/incomplete`, not a measured regression. Each step produces a content-addressed finding linked to the corresponding raw artifacts; no top-level pass substitutes for these findings.

1. **Freeze the reviewed inputs and attempt before timing.** Select the exact approved manifest and host-policy revision, source/control compositions, at most three primary claims and their endpoint/owner/cohort/unit/statistic, required coverage, samples, completion/state-reset semantics and fixed attempt budget. The experiment decision selects these, not this diagnostic. Record the shuffled six-block order before collection. Preserve prior attempts, partial outcomes and correction/restart authorization. Bind all of this to artifact hashes; changing it invalidates calibration applicability.
2. **Establish reconstructable custody and actual execution.** Verify source commits, complete dirty tracked/untracked input archives where applicable, dependency and build/toolchain/configuration inputs, harness/fixture identity and every actual executable role (server, client, microbinary, probe/peer and short-lived launcher/helper). Hash retained bytes, not just report strings. Reconstruct the archive and compare the report bindings. Account for symlinks, executable modes, dependency state and dynamic build/runtime inputs; an empty dirty digest does not archive anything. Compare identities at campaign/block boundaries and within each A/A series. Source differences between a declared baseline/candidate pair are expected; any A/A source/binary change is not. Missing runtime role/launch evidence cannot be repaired afterward with the checkout's present binary.
3. **Admit the approved host epoch and capture scope.** Independently compare required observations to the policy resolution, including exact kernel and usable memory, logical/physical CPUs, CPU policy and actual inherited affinity of measured processes. Check host-wide lock identity, boot/configuration continuity, all before/after campaign and block checks, valid finite nonnegative load within the approved ceiling, and no concurrent benchmark/build workload. Verify release/full/tracing-off and equal fixture, geometry, terminal configuration, work and completion. Merely requesting `0-7` is not affinity evidence. Missing, failed or stale observations/calibration stop admission. The current old memory pin and omitted kernel checks cannot implement the approved epoch unchanged.
4. **Strictly review raw records, not only budget-selected arrays.** Parse JSON with duplicate-key and nonfinite-constant rejection. Compare exact required scenario/profile/condition/metric sets and unique micro repetition indices, not only set membership or nonempty output. Verify schemas and real completed execution, including failed/interrupted runs that retained arrays. Check every applicable raw measurement for finite nonnegative numeric type excluding booleans, endpoint/unit agreement, and actual cardinality consistent with declared repetitions and the selected metric contract. Specifically include `outer_bytes`, profile resource distributions and workloads absent from the 79 paired checks. A genuinely one-time completion observation is not automatically required to have 100 samples, but cannot count as a repeated metric. Recompute validity from data; false validity, unsupported flags with fabricated arrays, blank unsupported reasons, or support drift do not authorize a pass. Required unsupported evidence blocks its claim. Supplemental primary p95 claims need the approved independent observations, not merely the manifest minimum.
5. **Run the existing validators on every role, including all A/A and baseline captures.** In a new derived-output directory run `validate_report.py --micro`, `validate_report.py --process` for both process/profile reports, and `check_regression.py` with all three reports and the selected manifest. Do not rely on candidate-only evaluation by the paired analyzer. Exit 2 from the budget analyzer is invalid evidence; exit 1 is an absolute-target failure to retain separately, not an excuse to skip it or relabel it a pass. Steps 2–4 remain required even when these commands exit zero.
6. **Verify independence, order and applicable direct controls.** Bind separate invocation/start/end/completion records, commands and state resets to all three A/A captures per source/configuration and all six fresh paired blocks per comparison. Reject reused calibration/candidate artifacts, missing blocks and substitutions. Equal values are allowed; different timestamps or hashes alone do not prove fresh execution. Check actual order against the predeclared three/three shuffle. For applicable direct comparisons, verify before < subject < after execution and complete valid raw direct controls, not merely the unordered task set or `completed` label. A Lemma-only claim does not need an invented competitor matrix. Runtime blocked-client backpressure, equal work and foreground overlap require capture-time evidence; source review and equal peer hashes cannot attest them.
7. **Recompute calibration, paired checks and absolute targets independently.** First bind successful three-capture unchanged-state calibration to each source/configuration and required supplemental metric. Execute `calibrate_regression.py` and inspect the complete manifest-owned ID/status set. Reject enforced noise failures; never use its suggested minimum floor to tune policy. For each valid paired block, run `compare_regression.py` with the correct independent baseline/candidate directories and selected manifest. Independently recompute raw nearest-rank statistics, every paired bound and every absolute/within-candidate target (the formulas and diagnostic arithmetic implementation in this asset show the procedure). Require exactly the expected unique IDs and compare every result field/status, not merely overall status. Retain diagnostics and supported/unsupported distinctions. The reviewed set is 79 paired IDs (6 micro, 33 process, 40 profile) and 80 absolute IDs; six paired IDs remain diagnostic. A prohibited paired regression blocks even with favorable absolute targets. An absolute miss stays explicit even when paired exit is zero; baseline absolute checks are needed to establish inheritance, and only the route decision can accept individual debt.
8. **Evaluate the predeclared claims and stopping rules separately.** Apply the approved practical floors, both applicable A/A spreads, all six strictly positive margins and the fixed claim budget/uncertainty rule from the policy resolution; retain each input/effect/margin and the minimum. Do not pool captures, replace bad blocks, choose primary metrics after outcomes, or label the manifest sample minimum as tail confidence. Scan raw native foreground endpoints for the approved repeated severe-tail trigger; p99's diagnostic status does not waive it. A noise failure, prohibited regression, admission failure or completion failure triggers the corresponding stop/diagnostic/restart rule, not selective rerunning. Runtime resource gaps remain explicit and cannot be traded against responsiveness.

### What cannot be supplied by this retrospective review

Both block measurement. Do not start real captures or silently add runtime instrumentation during this diagnostic.
